## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production texture asset for original detailed Minecraft fantasy creature meshes, not concept art. Generate ONE square diffuse material atlas: exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of sixteen equal square material swatches filling the entire image edge to edge. Orthographic flat materials, no perspective, no objects, no labels, no letters, no borders or gutters. Crisp hand-painted stylized voxel-RPG texture quality, deliberate pixel clusters and sharp material details, readable at small scale, no photography/no blurred gradients. All sixteen tiles unique, uniform ambient diffuse lighting. Tiles ordered left-to-right then top-to-bottom: 0 moss green goblin/orc leathery skin with subtle pores and scars; 1 coarse dark brown boar/bear fur with directional tufts; 2 silver-white wolf fur with blue-grey shadows; 3 copper olive reptile scales with clear individual plates. Row2: 4 aged ivory bone with cracks; 5 weathered burgundy-purple woven cloth with seams; 6 dark tempered steel armor with scratches and engraved borders; 7 old leather saddle and harness material with stitching and rivets. Row3: 8 black volcanic rock with vivid orange magma fissures; 9 translucent-looking glacial blue ice facets and frost etching, opaque diffuse; 10 abyssal violet obsidian with subtle blue veins; 11 mossy ancient bark with deep grooves. Row4: 12 antique gold engravings; 13 layered ochre-cream feathers with strong shafts and barbs; 14 bright cyan magical crystal facets; 15 nearly black charcoal smooth material. Colors rich but cohesive with an elegant dark fantasy game. Every tile must depict material only, no creatures or props. Desired square resolution 1024 or higher.
