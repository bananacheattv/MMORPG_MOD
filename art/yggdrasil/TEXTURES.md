# Matériaux des gardiens — Aeloria

Atlas original généré avec le tool intégré image_gen, puis copié sans retouche raster dans `material_atlas.png` et dans les ressources du mod. Les UV des modèles utilisent les seize matériaux de cet atlas. Aucun modèle 3D n’est fourni par cette image : la géométrie, les articulations et les animations sont créées séparément dans les projets Blockbench.

## Prompt de génération

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production diffuse material atlas for original Minecraft fantasy boss models. Create ONE square texture atlas, exactly a seamless 4 by 4 grid of equal square material swatches, all sixteen tiles edge-to-edge, no gutters, no labels, no lettering, no borders, no 3D objects, no perspective. Orthographic flat material surfaces filling every tile. Professional hand-painted pixel-art game texture style with crisp intentional pixel clusters, restrained highlights and visible material structure, compatible with premium Blockbench Minecraft creature art. No photographic noise, no smooth airbrush gradients. Each tile should be a repeatable organic or crafted material, not a picture of a creature. Tile order left to right, top to bottom: row 1: dark emerald beetle chitin overlapping angular shell plates with fine gilded hairline seams; bright jade leaf armor with visible branching veins; deep azure ray skin with fine star-like bioluminescent scale spots; turquoise coral dragon scales with pale calcified ridges. Row 2: warm amber segmented insect armor with worn edges; violet jellyfish membrane with delicate lavender filaments and luminous specks; gunmetal crab armor with rivets, scratches, inset seams and muted bronze trim; rich indigo-purple moth wing membrane with bold symmetrical pale vein patterns. Row 3: dark crimson serpent scales with individual highlighted edges; very dark ancient stone with carved gold filigree; ivory bone and horn with longitudinal striations and shaded fractures; dark burgundy monster mouth flesh with fibrous ridges. Row 4: richly engraved aged brass and gold; dark green-black bark with emerald glowing runic cracks; luminous pale opaline crystal facets with cyan-violet refractions; near-black obsidian enamel with cool subtle edge highlights. Palette cohesive, formidable ancient world-tree bosses. Uniform diffuse lighting only. Entire image must be directly usable as a UV texture sheet, not a labeled concept-art contact sheet. High quality, 1024 square if possible.
